# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One participant logging a set on a phone between exercises, often standing with one hand free. A future private group is secondary. The existing repository and URL remain stable.

## Product Purpose
A fast personal annual Push-Up Challenge tracker that confirms saved reps, separates training, official and off-season activity, and preserves previous years. Approved direction is in docs/vision.md and docs/strategy.md.

## Brand Personality
Direct, calm, encouraging. Keep orange associated with action and progress; avoid guilt or competitive pressure.

## Anti-references
No social-feed dashboard, crowded fitness gamification, unexplained achievement animations, empty leaderboard, or decoration that obscures save status.

## Design Principles
- Confirm persistence before claiming success.
- Keep logging reachable in one or two taps.
- Show the selected year and activity phase explicitly.
- Preserve useful existing controls and historical provenance.
- Show unknown official daily targets as pending.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, text enlargement to 200%, browser zoom, reduced motion, and usable small-phone controls. Validate at 320, 390 and 430px before implementation is accepted.
